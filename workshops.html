Our painting workshop

Coffee with
Canvas.

An afternoon at the studio where you paint something and take it home. Madhvi shows you how, step by step. You do not need to know how to paint. Most people who come never have.

What you paintA canvas, or something you can actually use: a cap, a tote, a clutch. We say which when the date is announced.
What is includedPaints, brushes, the thing you paint on, and coffee. Bring nothing.
Who comesMostly complete beginners. Friends, colleagues, and plenty of people on their own.
How longAn afternoon. You leave with the thing you painted, finished.
